SECTOR ANALYSIS

Industry Challenges

01

Supply Chain Bottlenecks

Limited downstream refining capacity forces miners to export raw concentrates at compressed margins, surrendering value-add opportunities to foreign processing facilities.

02

Limited Refining Capacity

North American refining infrastructure remains underdeveloped relative to extraction volume, creating systemic dependency on overseas processing and geopolitical supply risk.

03

Sustainability Requirements

Increasing pressure from regulators, investors, and offtake partners demands lower-emissions processing pathways, a challenge legacy pyrometallurgical infrastructure cannot economically meet.

04

Traceability Demands

EV manufacturers and battery producers require documented chain-of-custody data for critical minerals, a standard existing broker-led supply chains cannot provide at scale.
