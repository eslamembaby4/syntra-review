Stage 04: Syntra's Position
The Missing Link
North America produces significant raw minerals but ships concentrate to Asia for refining. Syntra builds domestic refining capacity to process those feedstocks inside the continent.
Feedstock Agnostic
Multi-Source Inputs
Syntra's proprietary process accepts concentrate from mines, manufacturing scrap, and recycled black mass, making it resilient to supply disruptions across all upstream stages.
Stage 07: Circular Economy
Closed-Loop Ready
As battery recycling scales, Syntra is positioned to process recycled black mass alongside virgin concentrate, enabling a true circular critical minerals economy in Canada.
Battery-Grade Output
Specification Certainty
Refined outputs meet IEC, ASTM, and OEM-specific purity requirements for lithium, cobalt, nickel, manganese, and copper. Ready for Stage 05 precursor manufacturing.
Defence & Aerospace
Sovereign Supply
Government and defence customers require domestic, traceable critical mineral supply. Syntra provides certified chain-of-custody refining from Canadian and allied sources.
2027 Production Cycle
Strategic Timing
Battery gigafactories in Ontario and Quebec require domestic refined inputs by 2026–2028. Syntra's timeline is aligned with North American EV supply chain localization mandates.
